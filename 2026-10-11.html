☀️ Big Story of the Day
Stelco starts laying off up to 500 workers in Hamilton, and Ottawa gives the owner until Oct 13 to answer

Stelco, the big Hamilton steel plant, is sending layoff notices to as many as 500 workers. The company blames the trade war with the U.S. (tariffs are taxes on imports that make Canadian steel more expensive to sell south of the border). Its U.S. parent, Cleveland-Cliffs, says workers could be recalled if a trade deal happens. Industry Minister Mélanie Joly has threatened legal action if the company does not honour its job commitments from the 2024 takeover and set an Oct 13 evening deadline for a plan (BNN Bloomberg).

Video-worthy because: it is 45 minutes from Toronto, it follows Canada losing 68,000 jobs in September, and there is a hard date (Oct 13) in two days. Caveat: the layoffs started Friday, so this is a 48-hour story, not brand new.

🎯 The One Thing
Every big bank has now raised mortgage rates in one week. CIBC went up Friday (+0.20 points on 5-year fixed) after RBC, BMO, National Bank, Scotiabank and TD, even though Canada just lost 68,300 jobs (Canadian Mortgage Trends).
