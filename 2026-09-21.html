🎯 The One Thing
Ontario's new rental rules kicked in today — if you're behind on rent, you now have half as much time to catch up.

As of today, September 21st, a batch of changes to Ontario's Residential Tenancies Act take effect. The notice period for late rent (the "N4") drops from 14 days to 7. Three late payments within 7 days of the due date over six months can now be labelled "persistent late payment," opening the door to a different eviction notice. Landlords using an N12 to move in themselves can skip paying a tenant one month's compensation if they give 120+ days' notice instead of the usual 60. And tenants who want to raise certain defences at a rent-arrears hearing generally now have to pay 50% of the claimed arrears up front to do it (CBC News, NOW Toronto).
