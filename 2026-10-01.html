☀️ Big Story of the Day
StatCan found 301,000 people it had missed, and the "population is shrinking" story fell apart

Statistics Canada revised its April 2026 population up by 301,008 people to about 41.7 million. Roughly 240,000 of them were non-permanent residents (temporary workers, students, asylum claimants). The earlier numbers had shown three straight quarters of decline; the revision calls it a brief pause. Ontario got nearly half the extra people, about 140,000 (StatCan, CBC, Open Data Canada summary).

Daniel Foch posted two videos on it overnight, arguing these people were already here, so it is not new demand (Foch video). Honest caveat: the StatCan release itself is dated Sept 23; today's pickup is the fresh part. I could not open the StatCan page directly from my tools, so numbers come from the Open Data Canada summary of it.

Video-worthy because: it reverses a headline many viewers saw, with one hard number.

Why you care: less new supply over time means fewer homes to rent or buy, so the 'prices will crash forever' story has a limit.
