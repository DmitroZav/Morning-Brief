☀️ Big Story of the Day
George Chuvalo, the immigrant's son who was never knocked down, has died at 89.

George Chuvalo — born Jure Cuvalo to Croatian immigrant parents who worked in Toronto's slaughterhouses and poultry factories — died Saturday, September 12, his 89th birthday. He grew up in The Junction, a working-class west-Toronto neighbourhood, and became Canada's five-time heavyweight boxing champion, fighting Muhammad Ali, Joe Frazier and George Foreman. In 93 professional fights against some of the greatest heavyweights ever, he was never once knocked down — Ali himself called him "the toughest guy I ever fought" (CP24, Wikipedia).

Why it's video-worthy: this isn't a real estate story, and it doesn't need to be. A factory workers' son from Herzegovina becomes a Canadian legend by refusing to go down, in the exact working-class Toronto neighbourhoods thousands of newcomers now call home. That's a story this audience feels in their bones.

🎯 The One Thing
August inflation held at 3% — it didn't get worse, and after weeks of rising bond yields, that's the win.

StatCan's August Consumer Price Index, released this morning, came in flat at 3.0% year-over-year — identical to July, and matching what economists expected. Food prices eased to 2.8%, the first time food inflation has dropped below 3% in 14 months. Core inflation (the number the Bank of Canada actually watches) stayed put too: CPI-median at 2.0%, CPI-trim at 1.9% — both right around target, even with oil spiking toward $108/barrel on a Saudi pipeline attack (Reuters via Investing.com, Globe and Mail).
