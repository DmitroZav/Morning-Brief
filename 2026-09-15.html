☀️ Big Story of the Day

On Sunday, Russian strikes hit close to a Warsaw-bound train and near Poland's border with Ukraine — inside or right up against NATO territory. NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte responded Monday, calling it a sign of Vladimir Putin's "desperation" and a deliberate attempt to sow fear among NATO members, and pledged the 32-nation alliance will increase support for Kyiv while also strengthening its own defenses along the eastern border with Russia (Al Jazeera, Washington Times).

Worth a direct, honest video for the Ukrainian-Canadian viewers who have family within driving distance of that border.

🎯 The One Thing
Canada's national home sales fell 6.9% in August — but the average price still went UP, and sellers just came off the sidelines.

CREA (the Canadian Real Estate Association) released its August numbers this morning. Nationally, 37,504 homes sold — down 6.9% from a year ago, and down 0.7% from July once you adjust for the usual seasonal patterns. But the national average price actually rose 0.6% year-over-year to $668,219. And here's the part worth watching: new listings jumped 3.3% month-over-month, snapping three straight months of fewer homes coming onto the market (CREA news release via GlobeNewswire, BNN Bloomberg).

🧠 Why It Matters (To You)

If you're thinking about selling: national sales are down, but your home hasn't lost value on paper — the average price is still up 0.6% from a year ago. The catch is competition is about to increase: new listings just jumped after three months of sellers holding back, so if you list, expect more company on the market. If you're buying: more listings means more choice and more leverage over the next few months — a good moment to be picky rather than rushed, which is exactly why Kevin Yu's red-flag video below is worth your two minutes. If you're a newcomer with family near Ukraine's border: today's NATO story isn't about your mortgage, but it's real, and it's okay for that to be the thing on your mind today too.
